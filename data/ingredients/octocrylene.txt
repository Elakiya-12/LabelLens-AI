Ingredient: Octocrylene

Category: Sunscreen

What it is:
Octocrylene is an organic UV filter used in sunscreen formulations.

Common uses:
It helps protect skin from UVB radiation and contributes to broad-spectrum sunscreen formulations when combined with other UV filters.

Potential benefits:
It helps provide UV protection and can also help stabilize certain sunscreen formulations.

Potential concerns:
Some people may experience irritation or sensitivity.

Typical product categories:
Sunscreens, SPF moisturizers, and other sun-protection products.

Important:
The overall level of sun protection depends on the complete sunscreen formulation.
