Ingredient: Niacinamide

Category: Skincare

What it is:
Niacinamide is a form of vitamin B3 commonly used as a skincare ingredient.

Common uses:
It is used in serums, moisturizers, sunscreens and other skincare products.

Potential benefits:
It may help support the skin barrier, improve the appearance of uneven skin tone and reduce the appearance of excess oil.

Potential concerns:
Some people may experience redness or irritation, particularly with higher concentrations.

Important:
Tolerance can vary between individuals and formulations.
