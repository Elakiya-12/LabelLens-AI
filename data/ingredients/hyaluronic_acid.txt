Ingredient: Hyaluronic Acid

Category: Skincare

What it is:
Hyaluronic acid is a substance that helps attract and retain water.

Common uses:
It is commonly used in moisturizers, serums and hydrating skincare products.

Potential benefits:
It helps improve skin hydration and can make skin feel smoother and more supple.

Potential concerns:
It is generally well tolerated, although irritation can depend on the complete product formulation.

Important:
Its performance depends on the formulation and other ingredients in the product.
