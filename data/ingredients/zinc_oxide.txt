Ingredient: Zinc Oxide

Category: Sunscreen

What it is:
Zinc oxide is a mineral UV filter commonly used in sunscreen products.

Common uses:
It is used to provide protection against UVA and UVB radiation.

Potential benefits:
It provides broad UV protection and is commonly used in mineral sunscreen formulations.

Potential concerns:
It may leave a white cast on the skin. Some people may experience mild irritation or sensitivity.

Typical product categories:
Mineral sunscreens, face sunscreens, baby sunscreens, and SPF cosmetics.

Important:
The protection provided depends on the concentration, formulation, and application of the sunscreen.
