Ingredient: Glycerine

Category: Skincare

What it is:
Glycerine is a humectant that attracts and helps retain water in the skin.

Common uses:
It is commonly used in moisturizers, cleansers, serums and other skincare products.

Potential benefits:
It helps keep skin hydrated and can improve the feeling of softness and smoothness.

Potential concerns:
Glycerine is generally well tolerated in skincare products. Individual formulations may cause irritation depending on other ingredients.

Important:
Its effect depends on the overall formulation and concentration.
