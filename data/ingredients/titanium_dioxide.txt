Ingredient: Titanium Dioxide

Category: Sunscreen

What it is:
Titanium dioxide is a mineral UV filter used in sunscreens.

Common uses:
It is used to help protect skin from ultraviolet radiation.

Potential benefits:
It can provide protection against UV radiation and is commonly used in mineral sunscreen formulations.

Potential concerns:
Some formulations may leave a visible white cast on the skin. Skin sensitivity or irritation can occur in some people.

Typical product categories:
Mineral sunscreens, face sunscreens, and cosmetics containing SPF.

Important:
Its performance depends on particle size, concentration, formulation, and how the sunscreen is applied.
