Ingredient: Salicylic Acid

Category: Skincare

What it is:
Salicylic acid is a beta hydroxy acid (BHA) commonly used in skincare products.

Common uses:
It is commonly used in products designed for acne-prone and oily skin. It can help remove dead skin cells from the surface of the skin and within pores.

Potential benefits:
Salicylic acid can help unclog pores, reduce the appearance of acne and improve the appearance of rough or uneven skin.

Potential concerns:
It may cause dryness, peeling, redness or irritation, particularly when used too frequently or at higher concentrations.

Typical product categories:
Cleansers, acne treatments, toners, serums and exfoliating products.

Important:
The effect and tolerability of salicylic acid depend on its concentration, formulation and frequency of use. Users with sensitive skin may need to introduce it gradually.
