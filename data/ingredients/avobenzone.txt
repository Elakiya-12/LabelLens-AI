Ingredient: Avobenzone

Category: Sunscreen

What it is:
Avobenzone is a chemical UV filter used in sunscreens.

Common uses:
It is used to provide protection against UVA radiation.

Potential benefits:
It helps protect skin from UVA exposure, which can contribute to premature skin aging and pigmentation.

Potential concerns:
Some people may experience skin irritation or sensitivity.

Typical product categories:
Sunscreens, moisturizers with SPF, and other sun-protection products.

Important:
Its effectiveness depends on the formulation and concentration of the sunscreen.
