Ingredient: Kojic Acid

Category: Skincare

What it is:
Kojic acid is a skin-brightening ingredient commonly used in skincare products.

Common uses:
It is commonly used to improve the appearance of hyperpigmentation, dark spots, and uneven skin tone.

Potential benefits:
Kojic acid can help reduce the appearance of pigmentation and brighten uneven skin tone.

Potential concerns:
It may cause dryness, redness, itching, or irritation in some people.

Typical product categories:
Serums, creams, spot treatments, and brightening products.

Important:
Patch testing is recommended because individual skin tolerance can vary.
